Settings live in the badge, effects in one tray, the mic only in Video.

  1. 0:08“Apple wouldn’t have these two things here… a separate thing, because it belongs to camera settings.”
    Resolution and frame rate left the controls. They live in Camera Settings, opened from the badge on the preview.
  2. 0:59“It’s redundant… it shows here but also in this overlay. Maybe you can edit it from the overlay.”
    The overlay is the control. The HD · 30 badge shows the format and opens its settings, so the format appears once.
  3. 0:21“Apple wouldn’t have so many of these in here either. It would be a bit cleaner.”
    From 11 controls to 4 under the preview: timer, shutter, folder, and the mic in Video. Mirror moved to settings; blur, brighten and ring light moved into one effects tray.
  4. 0:27“For photo, the microphone icon doesn’t make sense when you’re in photo mode.”
    The mic and its level meter appear only in Video. Switch to PHOTO and they’re gone.
  5. 0:52 / 1:03“This is more like editing… the blur effect is good, but maybe it’s in an effects overlay.”
    One effects button on the preview opens a tray over the picture with Portrait blur, Brighten and Ring light, each with labeled levels. You see the result while you change it.
  6. 0:41“The ring lighting… I’m not sure where that would be.”
    Ring light is in the effects tray (Off / Soft / Bright). Try Bright: the desktop’s edges light up, as on a real screen. The display choice stays on its right-click.
  7. 2:55 / 3:05“The icon for the camera is kind of weird… all the icons are much bigger. The icon for this looks weird too.”
    No more mystery glyphs. The camera picker is a named chip, 🎥 OsmoPocket3 ▾, on the preview. Blur gets a clear person-with-bokeh icon and a text label in the tray.
  8. 1:28“If you’re looking at a picture, this label, the whole thing, is very bad. Apple would show the images nicely.”
    The file-name pill is gone. The photo fills the frame, and the top bar shows only “Today · 5:37 AM” like Photos. Arrows sit at the edges, out of the picture’s way.
  9. 1:50“These are in the right place, but when you go to a video it immediately moves up, which is wrong.”
    Controls stay put for videos. The same top bar and edge arrows; the player sits in its own strip at the bottom.
  10. 2:04“I click the camera button and it takes a picture. It should just switch to the camera.”
    While viewing, the shutter means “back to camera”. It dims and says so; the next press takes the picture.
  11. 2:20“Show in Finder, open Preview… extra. Share or delete, only those two. Double-click shows the preview.”
    The viewer has Share and Delete only. Double-click the picture to open it in Preview or QuickTime.
  12. 3:10“Apple doesn’t really ask location like this, ‘Save Location’.”
    Location is a plain switch in Camera Settings, worded like Apple’s. macOS asks for permission once, the first time it’s on.
  13. 3:17 / 3:20“Don’t worry about video effects… open folder, why does that need to be in here? There’s already an icon for that.”
    Removed: macOS Video Effects and Open Folder are gone from the menus. The folder button next to the shutter does that.
  14. 3:31“Quit can be like a top bar, or quit can just stay here. The only option can be quit, or any other thing that we move here.”
    The ⋯ menu is gone. Quit is the last row of Camera Settings (⌘Q works too), next to the settings that moved there. Right-clicking the menu-bar icon still offers Quit.

Kept as is, from earlier rounds

  • Aspect picker with the rotate button that turns 9:16 into 16:9; right-click it for a camera mounted sideways.
  • Click to turn something on, right-click for its choices (timer 3 or 10 s, mic list, ring light displays).
  • The thumbnail strip, which you were using in the video.
